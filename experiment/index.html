Frequently Asked Questions

Does this verify identity or measure biometrics?

No. This experiment measures transient interaction patterns during your active session — such as reaction timing, cursor movement consistency, and typing rhythm. It does NOT ask for, collect, or inspect your identity, credentials, or biometric profiles. No behavioral profile or historical data is ever compiled.

Does this prove humanity or guarantee security?

No. The overall score is a probabilistic confidence estimate based solely on signals observed during the current session. It carries no legal weight, certification status, or guarantee against sophisticated automation. Scores represent confidence levels rather than absolute proof.

How is privacy protected and how does browser-only processing work?

All signal collection and scoring algorithms run completely in your browser's temporary memory. No data is transmitted to remote servers, written to cookies, or stored in local browser storage. Refreshing or leaving the page immediately discards all transient state and redirects to the Welcome screen.

Why is this an experimental demonstration and what are its limitations?

HUMNLABS is an exploratory research initiative investigating privacy-preserving human presence signals. Current limitations include sensitivity to input hardware differences, touch screen variations, and potential overlap with advanced automation tools. The methodology is continuously evaluated as an open research demonstration.
